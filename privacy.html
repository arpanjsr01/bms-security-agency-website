Privacy and data handling

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 July 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how BMS Security Agency handles information received through this website, direct business communication, and authorised operational tools that may use the WhatsApp Business Platform.

2. Information we may process

  • Business names, contact persons, phone numbers, email addresses, and enquiry details.
  • Authorised personnel names, work-contact numbers, assigned tasks, progress updates, and timestamps.
  • WhatsApp Business Account identifiers, phone-number identifiers, message identifiers, delivery status, and template information.
  • Message content and media metadata submitted through authorised business workflows.
  • Operational records required for reporting, follow-up, support, and security.

3. How information is used

  • Responding to enquiries and providing requested services.
  • Managing authorised task-allocation, reporting, follow-up, and workforce-coordination workflows.
  • Sending and receiving authorised business communications.
  • Providing management reports, operational alerts, support, and troubleshooting.
  • Protecting systems, preventing misuse, maintaining records, and complying with legal obligations.

4. WhatsApp Business Platform

Where a business authorises BMS to operate a WhatsApp Business Platform integration, BMS processes the minimum data required to provide the authorised service. Access is limited to approved business purposes and permissions granted through Meta's onboarding and business-management tools.

5. Sharing and sale of information

BMS does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with authorised service providers only when necessary to operate the service, maintain hosting, deliver communications, or comply with law.

6. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the authorised service, business records, security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations.

7. Security

BMS uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to limit unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Access, correction, and deletion

Eligible requests may be submitted using the instructions on the Data Deletion page.
