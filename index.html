About BMS

A single source for security and contractual manpower requirements

BMS Security Agency provides security services and contractual manpower to businesses, institutions, industrial units, healthcare facilities, events, and other organisations.

Personnel are selected and deployed according to the client's site, responsibilities, working conditions, schedules, and applicable legal or professional requirements.

Operational process

Site assessment, deployment planning, monitoring, and reporting

BMS assesses the client requirement, defines the relevant posts and responsibilities, selects suitable personnel, and coordinates deployment.

Regular and surprise checks, field-officer visits, management communication, and structured reporting support accountability across assignments.
