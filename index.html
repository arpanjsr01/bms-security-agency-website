About BMS

Security services built around accountability and dependable manpower

BMS Security Agency is a security and manpower-services business serving multiple clients. We deploy personnel according to the client's operational requirements and focus on dependable communication, clear task allocation, and responsible supervision.

Our services include security guards, gunmen, bouncers, bodyguards, and other manpower support. Requirements are assessed according to the site, assignment, working conditions, and level of responsibility involved.
