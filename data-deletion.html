Data Deletion Instructions

Individuals and authorised business representatives may request deletion of eligible data processed by BMS Security Agency.

How to submit a request

  1. Email bmssecurities7@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your name, relevant business name, the phone number or email connected to the service, and a brief description of the data concerned.
  3. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or one-time passwords.
  4. BMS may request reasonable proof that you are authorised to make the request.

Processing of the request

After identity and authority are reasonably verified, BMS will review the request and delete or anonymise eligible information within a reasonable period. Some information may be retained where required for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, contractual records, or legal claims.

WhatsApp and Meta data

Deleting data held by BMS does not automatically delete information retained independently by WhatsApp, Meta, the user's device, or another business. Separate requests may be required through those services.
